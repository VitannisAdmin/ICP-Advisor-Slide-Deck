The Advisor LTC Trap

Navigating large unknown costs in a highly emotional environment.
No "plan" risks reduced returns, alienating the next gen and ultimately losing the AUM.

Portfolio & Allocation Risks
  • Forced conservative allocation: Results in lost opportunity cost for a significant portion of assets.
  • Escalated Sequence of Returns risk: Unpredictable, heavy withdrawals create rapid portfolio drag.
Next Generation Risks
  • Negative first impression: The lack of a turnkey plan creates a volatile introduction to the heirs.
  • Emotional volatility: A highly emotional situation compounded by an unknown cost component.
  • Planning paralysis: Difficult to plan around an unknown cost with an emotional next generation.

The Solution: The Immediate Care Plan (ICP)

The ICP acts as a financial firewall, protecting the estate from high costs and suppressed yields.

Turnkey Funding

A single, one-time deposit handles lifetime logistics seamlessly. Guarantees lifetime payments, ensuring the family never worries.

Capital Protection

A known amount upfront, caps the cost and keeps a majority of AUM in normal allocations. Avoids forced defensive allocations and premature strategy exits.

Peace of Mind

Removes financial stress. Allows the family to focus on their loved one, not the financial burden.

The Decisive Advantage: Morbidity Underwriting

The Immediate Care Plan is built on a Single Premium Immediate Annuity (SPIA) chassis, but its engine is fundamentally different. It uses medical reality, not population averages.

Standard SPIA

Standard Assumptions

  • Basis: General population data
  • Inputs: Age, Gender, Location
  • UW Versions: Requires extensive paramed exams, EKGs, and lengthy manual APS reviews (1-2 months).
Result: An income offer based on average life expectancy.
Immediate Care Plan

Morbidity-Focused Underwriting

  • Basis: Individual health reality (LTC-driven decline).
  • Inputs: Medical records, doctor's reports, and a brief caregiver interview.
  • Process: Streamlined application, automated RX reports, no physical exams (1-2 weeks).
Result: Provides a 200% - 500% payout increase compared to a standard SPIA for clients receiving care.

Advisor Takeaways & Best Practices

1

Mitigate Sequence of Returns Risk

Drawing heavily from a portfolio during the early years of a care event creates sequence of returns risk. The ICP transfers this longevity and market risk to the insurer.

2

Portfolio Optimization

By carving out a known, imminent liability upfront, you can maintain the remaining assets in an appropriate, growth-oriented allocation.

3

Estate Preservation

By Year 6, using an ICP results in significantly more legacy assets for heirs, proving that a lump sum premium is far more capital-efficient than piece-meal self-funding.

Identifying the Right Clients

  • Children (POAs) managing an aging parent's care
